Publications

IEEE BIBM 2026 · Accepted · CCF-B

When Do Clinical Text Embeddings Help? A Controlled Utility Analysis for Multimodal ICU Prediction

Chenyang Wang et al.

A controlled study of 21,011 MIMIC-III ICU stays shows that patient-matched early-note embeddings add signal beyond structured time series, shuffled notes, random vectors, and documentation metadata. Selective Note Fusion uses notes for 72.4% of patients while improving AUROC to 0.901 and AUPRC to 0.579, demonstrating that clinical text is conditionally rather than uniformly useful.

2026

ICIC 2026 · Oral Presentation · Springer CCIS 3038

Bi-dimensional Plug-in Enhancement for Tabular Learning: Explicit Feature and Sample Interaction for House Price Prediction

Chenyang Wang, Xinyu Lu, Zhuoyu Zhang, Jiamo Hu, Chenghao Yuan, and Yichuan Liu

BDPIE combines sample-level neighborhood aggregation with feature-level LLM embedding augmentation. Across five real-estate benchmarks, it improves most learner–dataset pairs with a 7.52% average relative test gain.

Research Projects

Clinical IR Ongoing

TRACE

Task-conditioned guideline retrieval from patient profiles.

TRACE studies how patient context and downstream clinical tasks should shape retrieval from external medical guidelines, with task-specific evidence needs and both model-based and human relevance judgments.

Representation Learning Manuscript

LREF

Learning to rewrite from embedding failures.

LREF learns from systematic mistakes made by frozen encoders and rewrites inputs so that decision-relevant distinctions become more visible to a fixed embedding model and downstream classifier.

Healthcare AI Manuscript

NECA

Neighbor-event curve augmentation for ICU deterioration prediction.

NECA augments early ICU measurements with trajectories from clinically similar patients to test whether retrieved longitudinal evidence improves early deterioration prediction.
