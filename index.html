Publications

2026

IEEE BIBM 2026 · Oral Presentation · CCF-B

When Do Clinical Text Embeddings Help? A Controlled Utility Analysis for Multimodal ICU Prediction

Chenyang Wang et al.

A controlled study of 21,011 MIMIC-III ICU stays shows that patient-matched early-note embeddings add signal beyond structured time series, shuffled notes, random vectors, and documentation metadata. Selective Note Fusion uses notes for 72.4% of patients while improving AUROC to 0.901 and AUPRC to 0.579, demonstrating that clinical text is conditionally rather than uniformly useful.

2026

ICIC 2026 · Oral Presentation · Springer CCIS 3038

Bi-dimensional Plug-in Enhancement for Tabular Learning: Explicit Feature and Sample Interaction for House Price Prediction

Chenyang Wang, Xinyu Lu, Zhuoyu Zhang, Jiamo Hu, Chenghao Yuan, and Yichuan Liu

BDPIE combines sample-level neighborhood aggregation with feature-level LLM embedding augmentation. Across five real-estate benchmarks, it improves most learner–dataset pairs with a 7.52% average relative test gain.

Research Projects

Clinical IR 05/2026 — Present

TRACE

Task-conditioned guideline retrieval from patient profiles.

TRACE segments discharge summaries into section-specific queries for three transition-of-care needs across MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV. Against 323 cardiovascular and metabolic guidelines, it reaches nDCG@10 ≈ 0.52 and outperforms whole-profile, compressed-query, multi-paraphrase, and task-agnostic baselines; judgments were validated through LLM evaluation and blinded human annotation.

Clinical NLP 05/2026 — 08/2026

SilentEHR

Auditing closed-world extrapolation in frozen patient representations.

A matched 11,466-example benchmark across 16 clinical concepts and 80 patient backgrounds reveals that multiple frozen encoders map 91–99% of unmentioned cases to Refuted despite 96–99% accuracy on explicit evidence. Explicit Unknown supervision sharply reduces this failure with minimal known-case accuracy loss.

Healthcare AI 04/2026 — 10/2026

NECA

Neighbor-event curve augmentation for ICU deterioration prediction.

NECA augments the first six hours of ICU measurements with trajectories from similar patients for 6/12/24/48-hour prediction across five deterioration tasks. It yields about 12% relative AUROC improvement for neurological deterioration and stable 3–7% gains on the remaining tasks across tabular and sequence models.

Representation Learning 06/2026 — 08/2026

LREF

Learning to rewrite from embedding failures.

LREF generates task-relevant semantic views from systematic frozen-encoder failures and concatenates them with the original representations without encoder fine-tuning. It improves accuracy by approximately three percentage points and transfers to clinical text classification and low-resource medical NLP.
