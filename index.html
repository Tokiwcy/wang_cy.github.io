Multimodal ICL Ongoing

BehaviorICL

Behavior-aware demonstration retrieval for multimodal in-context learning.

Instead of retrieving demonstrations solely because they look similar to a query, this project asks whether we can retrieve examples that are similar to how the target VLM decides. The goal is to use model behavior and decision-relevant evidence to select demonstrations that better steer few-shot prediction.

Clinical IR Manuscript

TRACE

Task-conditioned guideline retrieval for EHR decision support.

TRACE studies how patient context and downstream clinical tasks should shape retrieval from external medical guidelines. The project evaluates dense and hybrid retrieval under task-specific evidence needs, with both model-based and human relevance judgments.

Representation Learning Manuscript

LREF

Learning to rewrite from embedding failures.

LREF improves frozen text representations by learning from systematic embedding failures. Instead of fine-tuning the encoder, it rewrites inputs to make decision-relevant information more visible to a fixed embedding model and downstream classifier.

EmbeddingsText classificationFailure analysisFrozen models
Healthcare AI Manuscript

NECA

Neighbor-event curve augmentation for ICU deterioration prediction.

NECA augments early ICU measurements with trajectories from clinically similar patients. The central question is whether retrieved longitudinal evidence can improve early deterioration prediction across multiple outcomes and model families.
